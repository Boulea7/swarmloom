`--cue <at>:live` goes live at a chosen time, for example on a beat (`26b:live`), instead of 1.2 s after the final chord; it also works with `--no-live`, and an unfinished build completes at once. The halo finale fades out while the cursors leave instead of vanishing in one frame.
