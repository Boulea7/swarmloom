A name tag no longer stays behind at the edge of the frame when its cursor is outside it. A collaborator joining or leaving through a side edge, or a tour cursor past the top or bottom edge, used to leave its name pinned to the edge with no cursor beside it. Once the cursor's tip is off the frame its tag keeps its usual offset and leaves with it, fading out as the cursor art goes.
