The watch's QR dialog is a status for screen readers: a link read after it opens (a local file, pasted text, a handoff) says "getting the link ready", then that the code is ready or why the item cannot open on a phone. The dialog is named "Open on phone" and described by its caption. Small and round faces keep the caption under a code off screen rather than removing it; "getting the link ready" and the notices show in the code's place on every face.
