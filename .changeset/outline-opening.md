`intro: "outline"` (CLI `--intro outline`) opens without the file icon: up to three cursors (fewer when `agents` is smaller) walk in and draw the largest shapes in view, two each, and the rest of the swarm joins once all of them are taken. `--intro none` starts the build at once.
