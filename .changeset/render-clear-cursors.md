Cursors cover less of the text they work on. `labels: "none"` (`--labels none`) also leaves out the status bubbles ("running"), so the cursors carry no text. A cursor that types rests its I-beam just left of the text box and hangs its name tag below left of it, and a size badge takes the nearest spot that no name tag or status bubble covers.
