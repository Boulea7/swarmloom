The watch face keeps the build in view. With `?round=1` the stage plays inside the square that fits the circle, so the circle no longer crops the stage's corners and edges, and the watch draws no rulers or size badges. While a build plays or is paused, the controls sit small along the bottom on a dark backing, and they fade 0.6 s after play or resume instead of 2.6 s.
